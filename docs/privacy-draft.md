# Draft for hoopsmatic.com/privacy: Tier Lists

> **Tier Lists.** When you publish a tier list, we store the template it is for, where you put each player, when you published it and the name you choose to show (if any). Each published list gets its own link, which anyone with the link can open, and it counts toward that template's fans' consensus (the average of everyone's lists). To count one vote per person and to limit abuse, your browser keeps a random ID that we create. We store only a scrambled form of it (a keyed hash), which cannot be turned back into the ID or linked to you; if you publish again for the same template, your newest list is the one that counts. We do not ask for or store your email or an account, and we do not store your IP address. To limit abuse, we count lists published per network with a code that changes every day and is deleted after two days. Names pass a word filter before they are shown. Published lists stay up until you ask us, or we decide, to remove them; to ask, email hoopsmatic@hoopsmatic.com with the list's link. The consensus totals contain nothing about any one person. Your browser also keeps your list in progress and the name you last used, on your device only.

Matches what tier-lists-server stores (top of hoopsmatic-worker/tier-lists-server/src/index.js) and what the page keeps in localStorage (public/app.js: `tl-device`, `tl-rank-<slug>`, `tl-name`, `tl-mine`). Check it against the live Deep Cut, Puzzle Maker and crossword paragraphs before publishing.
